# Audio licences

**Rule: every track and SFX pack gets a row here at the time you download it, not later.**

"Royalty-free" does not mean "free to embed in a game". Standard tiers on many libraries cap
copies or exclude game embedding outright. Verify the licence explicitly permits **video game
use** before a file enters this repo.

## Phase 2 (after wishlist signal)

Commission 8–12 original tracks. 2026 indie rates: ~$200–$1,000 per finished minute, or
~$500–$2,000 per track. Brief the composer with a playable build, not a design document.

## SFX priority

Deckbuilders live or die on five sounds. Get these right before anything else:

1. Card draw
2. Card play
3. Hit impact
4. Block gain
5. Enemy intent reveal
